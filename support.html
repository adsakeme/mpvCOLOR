Help & Support

Questions about mpvCOLOR? Email micropixelversecoloring@gmail.com. Include your device model, operating system, app version and a short description of the issue. Never send account passwords.

Coloring and mini games

Use the in-game tutorials to learn each activity. Available content depends on the version installed on your device.


Backup and restore

Open Settings and the game-data controls. iOS uses iCloud. Android uses Google Play Games Saved Games where configured. Connect to the internet and use the same platform account that created the backup. Restore replaces current local progress. Android and iOS game backups are separate. Android can also restore eligible app data through its separate system backup service, depending on your device settings.


Delete a cloud backup

On Android, open Settings > Game Data, choose Restore to open the Google Play Games actions, then select and confirm Delete Backup. Use the same Play Games profile that created the backup and allow Google Play Games to sync. On iOS, open the game-data controls and confirm the iCloud delete-backup action.

This clears the selected in-game cloud backup. Local progress, Android system backups, platform accounts and profiles, and advertising data are managed separately. Platform retention policies apply. To remove local Android data, use your device’s app storage settings. Manage any Android system backup separately in device settings. For Play Games profile data, follow Google’s steps to delete data for a specific game.

For help with a deletion or privacy request, email micropixelversecoloring@gmail.com. Include the platform and the type of data you want to remove. Please keep account credentials private. See the privacy policy’s deletion section for details.

Advertising privacy choices
