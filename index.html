About

Bring a pixel universe to life

Follow the numbers, add color and reveal your next pixel character. Discover a collection of 200 character cards, from heroes and fantasy characters to animals, robots and more.

Then explore another side of your collection with Card Battle and a variety of mini games. Take a relaxing coloring break or try a new challenge — all in mpvCOLOR.

Our pixel universe keeps growing. New characters and mini games will be added in future updates, giving you more to color, collect and play.
