Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

mpvCOLOR is an ad-supported color-by-number game. The game does not require an account and the developer does not ask users to provide a name, email address, phone number, or other direct contact information to play. Advertising services integrated into the app may automatically process limited device, usage, diagnostic, and advertising information as described below.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the mpvCOLOR mobile application (“Application”) for Android and iOS, provided by mpvgamestudio (“Service Provider”, “we”, “us”, or “our”). The Application is provided as an ad-supported service and is intended for use “AS IS”. This policy also describes the accompanying mpvCOLOR developer website where expressly stated below.

2. Information processed by the Application

You do not need to create an account or submit personal contact information to use the Application. Game progress, completed characters, and preferences are stored locally on your device until you choose an available cloud backup feature. Optional backups use Apple iCloud on iOS and Google Play Games Saved Games on Android. Availability depends on your installed version and platform configuration.

The Service Provider does not receive your name, postal address, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise GPS location, payment information, or health information through normal gameplay.

Optional cloud backups

When you choose Backup, the app sends saved game progress and settings to your platform account’s app-private storage: Apple iCloud on iOS or Google Play Games Saved Games on Android. Saved data can include coloring progress, collection and unlock state, in-game balances, mini game records and preferences. Backup and restore require an internet connection and the relevant platform account. The app does not ask for your Apple or Google account password.

Backups are initiated through the game’s settings. Restore replaces the progress on the current device. iCloud and Google Play Games backups are separate; this feature does not provide transfer between iOS and Android. Apple and Google process platform account and service information under their own privacy policies.

See Apple’s Privacy Policy and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Deleting saved data and backups

In Settings, open the game-data / backup controls and choose the available delete-backup action. This removes or clears the app’s cloud backup on the selected platform; it does not erase progress already stored on this device or another device. On Android, the game clears the saved-game slot and Google Play Games synchronizes the change when connected. On iOS, the game removes its iCloud backup value and iCloud synchronizes the change.

Removing local app data or uninstalling the app does not by itself delete a cloud backup. Local removal options depend on your operating system, and operating-system backups may restore app data later. Use the in-game delete-backup control separately. If you need help, contact micropixelversecoloring@gmail.com. Do not send passwords or copies of private account credentials.

  • diagnostic information, including non-user-related crash logs;
  • performance information, such as launch time, hang rate, and energy usage; and

The precise information processed can depend on your device, operating system, region, privacy selections, the type of ad delivered, and Google’s current service configuration.

For more information, please review Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services .

5. Personalized ads and your choices

Advertising behavior depends on your device, region, permissions and the advertising service configuration. You can review available advertising controls through your device and Google account settings. Non-personalized and limited advertisements may still process an IP address, device and app information and fraud-prevention signals. On iOS, the tracking permission described below is controlled through Apple’s system prompt and settings. This permission is separate from any advertising consent that may be required in your region.

You may review Google advertising controls at Google My Ad Center. Additional privacy and advertising controls may also be available in your device settings.

6. App Tracking Transparency on iOS

On supported Apple devices, the Application may display Apple’s App Tracking Transparency permission request before accessing the device’s advertising identifier or performing tracking as defined by Apple. You may allow or deny this request. Denying tracking permission does not prevent you from playing the game, although the ads shown may be less personalized.

You can review or change this choice in iOS or iPadOS under Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

7. Location information

The Application does not request or collect precise GPS location. Advertising services may use an IP address to estimate a general location, such as a country or city-level area, for advertising, measurement, security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance.

8. Sharing and service providers

The Service Provider does not sell personal information directly. Information may be processed by Google and its advertising partners for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy. Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect legal rights, to investigate fraud or security issues, or as part of a business transfer subject to applicable law.

We require third-party service providers that receive user data through the Application to provide the same or equivalent protection of that data as described in this policy and required by applicable platform rules. We use Apple and Google services subject to their applicable privacy and data-protection terms. Provider privacy links above explain their processing and privacy controls.

9. Data retention

Locally stored game progress remains on your device until it is removed through the Application, cleared through the operating system, or the Application is uninstalled. Information processed by Google is retained in accordance with Google’s own policies, applicable legal requirements, user choices, and service settings.

Cloud game progress remains until you replace or delete the backup, subject to the platform provider’s synchronization, account and retention policies. If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to respond and keep it only as needed to handle the request or meet applicable legal obligations.

10. Children’s privacy and age ranges

mpvCOLOR is intended for a mixed audience, including children and adults. In the updated Android version, a neutral age-range screen helps apply appropriate advertising protections. It asks for an age range, not an exact birth date. The selection is stored locally on the device and is not included in the app’s cloud game-progress backups. Players can continue without selecting an age range and receive the more protective treatment.

On Android, players under 13 and players of unknown age receive child-directed advertising treatment. Players aged 13–17 also receive conservative under-age-of-consent advertising treatment. These groups do not receive interest-based personalized advertising or remarketing. Android ad requests use a general-audience content rating limit. The relevant age-treatment flags are provided to Google; the app does not send a birth date or a separately maintained age profile to us.

For adult Android players, Google’s consent message is displayed where required before advertisements can be requested. Where Google requires a privacy-options entry point, Settings > Ad Privacy Choices allows the player to revisit their choices. Declining personalized advertising does not prevent access to the game; advertisement availability depends on Google’s resulting privacy settings. Availability of this flow depends on the installed app version.

We do not ask children to provide names, contact details or other direct personal information to play. Platform sign-in for cloud backup is optional and subject to Apple’s or Google’s account eligibility and parental controls. Parents and guardians may contact micropixelversecoloring@gmail.com about privacy or deletion requests. Advertising and platform services may still process the limited information described elsewhere in this policy to deliver permitted services, secure them and prevent fraud.
